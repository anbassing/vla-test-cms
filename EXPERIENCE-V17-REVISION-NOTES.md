# Visit Locals — Experience Page Revision Based on v17

This build uses the v17 site as the base and applies the requested Experience page revisions.

- Native Calendar moved directly below Archipelagic Atlas; 10 seasonal choices, 4 visible initially.
- Seasonal cards open detailed experience modal with itinerary and CTA.
- Destination cards link to the existing 8 destination pages.
- Destination pages retain their travel experiences and now also include the six existing Business Mobility journeys.
- Curated Journeys expanded to 10, 5 visible initially. Modal label uses “Archipelagic Itinerary”.
- Archipelagic Passions auto-select the chosen journey in the planner and scroll to the form.
- Editorial Dispatch expanded to 10 field stories, 4 visible initially.
- Business bridge replaced the sample executive journey with the six Business page mobility journeys.
- Institutional Integrity section has scroll reveal + hover animation.
