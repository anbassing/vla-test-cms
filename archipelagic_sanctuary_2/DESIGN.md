## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the distinction of an elite sovereign hospitality gateway and high-end Indonesian cultural conduit. It serves affluent global voyagers, cultural patrons, and discreet cross-border investors seeking authentic sanctuary, curated estates, and rare sovereign access across the Indonesian archipelago. The interface rejects standard transactional travel aggregators and sterile enterprise SaaS tropes in favor of an artisanal, editorial publication aesthetic reminiscent of architectural monographs and bespoke travel compendiums.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **High-End Editorial Luxury** with **Warm Organic Tactility**:
- Broad expanses of parchment-tinted negative space convey unhurried confidence and cultural provenance.
- Typographic hierarchy behaves like a curated art journal, utilizing classical proportioning, fine hairlines, and deliberate rhythm.
- Imagery is treated with sovereign dignity: full-bleed vistas, deckle-edge micro-details, and muted contrast layers that evoke natural limestone, wet teak, volcanic basalt, and equatorial mist.
- Micro-interactions are deliberate, weighted, and graceful, entirely devoid of aggressive gamification or transactional urgency.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the Indonesian landscape: the depths of the Flores Sea, highland rainforest canopies, volcanic soil, and woven natural fibers.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Sovereign Navy (`#1c1a56`)**: The cornerstone tone used for primary navigational anchors, signature action states, and high-impact editorial moments. Complemented by soft hover states and deep midnight contrast.
- **Secondary Moss & Canopy (`#186b38`)**: Evokes the dense jungle interiors and heritage botanical reserves of Bali, Sumatra, and Java. Used for botanical tags, verification insignias, and sanctuary highlights.
- **Tertiary Earth & Taupe (`#c6a751`)**: Derived from aged teak, hand-thrown terracotta, and natural rattan. Employed for fine framing lines, dates, geographic coordinates, and metadata markers.
- **Neutral Grounds (`#575445`)**: A warm, luminous earthy baseline that replaces stark digital white, enriched for a more tactile, parchment-rich environment. Mid-ground surfaces rest on complementary tactile tones.
- **Ink Typography**: A near-black navy-charcoal that provides rich, legible contrast against warm grounds without the digital harshness of pure black.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances literary authority with contemporary Indonesian hospitality:

- **Headlines (`Newsreader`)**: Set with generous leading and natural tracking. It lends a bookish, cultured presence to villa titles, cultural chronicles, and sovereign partnership manifestos. Italic variations are deployed sparingly for regional terms, dialect notes, and narrative interludes.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers clean, humanist legibility with soft geometric nuances. It grounds technical particulars, booking itineraries, and fiscal overviews without competing against the editorial display font.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy**: Always presented in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to emulate archival catalogue folios and bespoke stationery.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces high-end editorial proportions, employing expansive margins that let narrative content breathe.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: Uses a fluid grid locked inside an expansive max-width container (1440px). Gutters (`3rem`) and outer margins (`6rem`) give sections room to frame photography and long-form narrative text. Asymmetric column spans establish pacing.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Gutters shift to `2rem` and outer canvas margins to `3rem`. Content stacks naturally while preserving generous interstitial breaks.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Single-column narrative flow with `1.5rem` gutters and outer canvas margins. Full-bleed imagery breaks across the edges to maximize visual scale on small displays.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical cadence relies on substantial space blocks (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) between narrative components. Content must never appear congested; density is an intentional counter-signal to luxury.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids high-contrast drop shadows and artificial floating layers. Depth is established through **natural material layering and tonal transitions**:

- **Tonal Stepping**: The page foundation sits on warm neutral grounds. Content modules and card containers elevate by shifting tones or sinking into deep panels, evoking stone, parchment, and polished wood.
- **Hairline Framing**: Separation between modules is achieved via ultra-fine hairline strokes, referencing fine architectural drawings and luxury prints.
- **Ambient Illumination**: Where interactive elevation is necessary, use a singular, deeply diffused ambient shadow tinted with warm taupe and slate.
- **Atmospheric Scrims**: Imagery overlays use a soft vertical gradient rather than solid black scrims, preserving the natural color spectrum of tropical foliage and marine water.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Rounded (`2`)** corner geometry, creating a balanced, refined silhouette across interactive elements and containers while maintaining structural integrity.

- Baseline UI elements (interactive buttons, input fields, badges) use clean, modern radiuses.
- Medium components feature moderate curves, maintaining crisp alignment with the editorial grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Deep Navy (`#1c1a56`) surface with light typography in `label-md`. Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover transition shifts smoothly with an organic 300ms easing.
- **Secondary (Editorial Outline)**: Transparent background bounded by a `1px` hairline in `#c6a751` (Earth Gold) with ink text. On hover, fills subtly with warm neutral undertones.
- **Text Action**: Unadorned `Newsreader` italic or `Plus Jakarta Sans` with an animated hairline underline offset by `4px`.

### Chips & Badges
- Used for sovereign jurisdictions, botanical classifications, and access levels.
- Typography: `label-sm` in `#186b38` or `#c6a751`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(198, 167, 81, 0.2)`. Padding: `4px 10px`.

### Editorial Cards
- **Sanctuary & Estate Cards**: Surface is bordered by a faint edge. Image aspect ratio standardizes to 4:5 (portrait) or 16:10 (architectural landscape). Includes category tag in `label-sm`, title in `Newsreader` (`headline-sm`), and geographic coordinates in muted taupe. Hover state applies a micro-zoom to the imagery (1.02 scale) and elevates via ambient warm shadow.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Inputs feature an ivory or warm neutral background sitting on an earth-toned baseline border (`1px solid #575445`).
- Focus state thickens the bottom border to `2px solid #1c1a56` without harsh outline rings.
- Placeholder text in subdued taupe-charcoal.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes: Small custom squares with soft radiuses. Unchecked: `1px solid #575445`. Checked: `#1c1a56` fill with a micro-tick.
- Radios: Concentric rings featuring an outer hairline border and an inner forest green dot (`#186b38`).

### Lists & Archival Tables
- Sovereign business dossiers and travel itineraries are structured as horizontal ledger rows separated by delicate `1px` hairlines.

### Bespoke Additions: Curated Itinerary Ledger & Sovereign Provenance Seal
- **Provenance Seal**: A circular, delicate SVG insignia combining archipelagic longitude/latitude marks with the regional sanctuary code.
- **Editorial Pull-Quote**: A centered layout module using `Newsreader` Italic at `36px`, bookended by double hairlines in `#c6a751` at 20% opacity.
